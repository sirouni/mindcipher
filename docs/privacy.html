Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Mind Cipher ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Mind Cipher does not collect, store, or transmit personal information in the background. All game progress, achievements, and settings are stored locally on your device. Optional feedback you choose to send is described below.

Game Center

Mind Cipher integrates with Apple's Game Center for leaderboards. Game Center is managed by Apple and subject to Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not access or store your Game Center data on our servers.

In-App Purchases

Mind Cipher offers in-app purchases processed entirely by Apple. We do not collect or have access to your payment information.

Data Storage

All game data (progress, scores, settings) is stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage mechanisms. We do not operate any servers or cloud services that store your data.

Feedback

If you choose to send feedback, you can open a public GitHub issue at github.com/sirouni/mindcipher or email sirouni@msn.com. The app does not submit this for you; it only opens GitHub or your mail app when you tap. GitHub issues are public and associated with the GitHub account you use (you may sign in with Google or Apple). Please do not include information you want to keep private.

Third-Party Services

Mind Cipher does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. Optional feedback may use GitHub or your email provider, which have their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Mind Cipher does not knowingly collect any information from children. The app is suitable for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: sirouni@msn.com
