FAQ

  • How does Lie Mode work? — In each round, one of the feedback indicators is fake. Use logic to find the contradiction and deduce the real code.
  • How do I use Smart Notes? — Tap a cell once for ✗ (eliminated), twice for ✓ (confirmed), three times to clear.
  • Can I restore my PRO purchase? — Go to Settings → Restore Purchases.
  • How does Challenge a Friend work? — Complete a level, tap "Challenge a Friend" to generate a share link that your friend can open.
